PhD Candidate, Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia.

My fields are health and labour economics. My job market paper studies wage floors for nurses and their consequences for hospitals and patients. I also worked on women's health with the HER-BC team at the Women's Health Research Institute.

You'll find my papers on the research page and more details in my CV.
